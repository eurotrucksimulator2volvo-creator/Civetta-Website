About Automobili Civetta

Crafting iconic Italian performance machines inspired by heritage, passion, and innovation.

Our Story

Automobili Civetta began when Antonio Civetta, a successful agricultural equipment manufacturer, after purchasing several Burnside vehicles, became dissatisfied with the frequent clutch failures. After a toxic argument with the CEO of Bruckell, he set out to create his own line of superior automobiles.

From the iconic Bolide to the ultra-modern Scintilla, our vehicles are designed to deliver emotion, prestige, and unforgettable performance.

Design Philosophy

We believe design should captivate from every angle. Every line, surface, and detail is crafted to create a powerful visual signature while enhancing aerodynamic performance.

Engineering Excellence

Our engineers focus on precision, balance, and driver engagement. Lightweight construction, powerful drivetrains, and refined chassis tuning make Civetta cars as compelling on the road as they are on the track.

The Civetta Legacy

At Civetta, every car is part of a legacy built on ambition and craftsmanship. We invite collectors and enthusiasts to experience the bold spirit of a brand that embraces tradition while shaping the future of performance automobiles.
