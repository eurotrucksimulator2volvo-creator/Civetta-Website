Powertrain

The Scintilla's powertrain is a masterpiece of engineering, combining the raw power of its naturally aspirated 5.0L V8 engine with a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission and an electronic Clutchex AWD system* for an exhilarating driving experience.

*Disclaimer: The Clutchex AWD system is only available on higher trims and may vary in performance based on driving conditions.
