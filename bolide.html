The Civetta Bolide

A historical model from the 1980s, named after the Italian word for "race car," remembered as a striking expression of late-century Italian automotive ambition.

Historical Note

The Bolide emerged during an era when automotive design was becoming increasingly theatrical, and Civetta’s work reflected that shift with bold lines and a strong sense of character.

"The car slides as if it was on ice." — Antonio Civetta

"Aerodynamics are for people who can't build engines. - Antonio Civetta"

It was not merely a car of its time, but a record of the ambitions and tastes of an era that prized spectacle as much as performance.

In historical perspective, the Bolide stands less as a modern performance machine and more as a cultural artifact: a reminder of how designers once imagined speed, style, and prestige in a very different language.

The Bolide became massively popular as soon as it launched. Rock stars, royalty, and the rich all wanted to get their hands on one of these beautifully crafted machines.
